## Species / class

Marker-dependent community: fishes (12S), eukaryotes (18S V9), optional COI invertebrates. Bound the first box to **one coastal system** relevant to a future wedge (Willapa/Puget Sound shellfish waters **or** a GOM statistical-area box **or** a CA/OR port box — founder still UNRESOLVED). Do not mix boxes.

## Mechanism

Sterile filtration of known volume at pre-registered stations and depths → preservation → metabarcoding + contamination controls → occupancy model with **hydrodynamic transport as a covariate, not an afterthought**. Copies/L may be recorded but **are not a biomass estimate**.

Physical principle: shed DNA persists hours–days, advects, decays ([Sassoubre et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.6b03114); [Scriver et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1002/edn3.405)).

## Evidence (cited; not claimed as FishAI skill)

- Programme ambition: [OBON](https://obon-ocean.org/), [Ocean Decade OBON](https://oceandecade.org/actions/ocean-biomolecular-observing-network-obon/).
- Autonomous sampling exists at site scale (MBARI ESP/FIDO; WHOI Oceanic sampler) — **not** a global mesh.
- Negative: eDNA concentration **need not** track invertebrate biomass ([*Carcinus* 2022](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12862-022-01969-z)).

## Requirements

- University/agency lab with blank discipline; vessel or dock access; 1 L–5 L filtration protocol locked **before** sampling; negative/positive controls; primer list; DUA if on farms.
- Optional: CTD, IFCB, or nets on the same casts.
- **Do not** wait for ESP fleets to start this occupancy test.

## Coverage

- Spatial: 8–20 stations in **one** estuary/shelf box, 1–3 depths.
- Temporal: ≥2 seasons (e.g. spring + late summer).
- This is **not** global coverage. Global mesh remains unfunded and unbuilt.

## Latency

Lab metabarcoding: **weeks**. In situ qPCR (if a partner already owns ESP): **hours–day** for targeted taxa only. Observatory product latency must be labeled; do not advertise real-time census.

## Minimum viable experiment

1. Pre-register: target = **detection / occupancy**, success metric = better than seasonal climatology of occurrence from nets/optics; **failure** if copies/L vs biomass \(r\) is used in copy.
2. Same-day paired water + net/IFCB at ≥8 stations × ≥2 surveys.
3. Report primer bias, blanks, and a simple transport-distance sensitivity (OpenDrift particles from station).

## Ground truth

Independent gear: plankton nets, beach seines, IFCB for microbes/phytoplankton, farm presence for sessile cultured taxa. Ground truth is **presence/effort**, not landings.

## FP / FN

| | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| FP | Contamination, downstream DNA, dead tissue, primer bleed | Blocs, blanks, transport model, PCR controls |
| FN | Inhibition, volume, decay, missing markers | Inhibition tests, volume curve, multi-marker |

## Legal / privacy / eco

- Coarsen listed-species detections to policy grain; **no** public pins.
- Nagoya/ABS/CARE if sampling Indigenous waters.
- Ecological harm low vs trawls; false invasion alerts can still cause harm — human review before alerts.

## Economics

ESTIMATE: \(1\times10^5\)–\(2\times10^5\) for two-season paired design (boat time + sequencing + analysis). Autonomous 10-node mesh: \(10^6\)–\(10^7\) — **out of MVE**.

## Kill criteria

1. Product or report language treats eDNA as \(N\) or 24–72 h abundance.  
2. Occupancy skill ≤ climatology after two seasons.  
3. Repeated blank failures.  
4. Inability to coarsen listed-species hits.

## Scale path

MVE occupancy → partner farms/ferries (`H-4.10`, `H-4.16`, `H-4.18`) as **nodes** → OBON-aligned metadata → only then discuss mesh density. Never skip calibration of decay/transport per water mass.
