## Species / class

Platforms: elephant, Weddell, hooded and other seals in MEOP. Quantity of interest for FishAI: **water temperature (especially subsurface/bottom-relevant profiles)**, not seal abundance.

## Mechanism

Download MEOP-CTD (version 2024-03-08: 872,119 profiles) / AniBOS feeds; QC vs Argo/ship; use as prior for thermal habitat (lobster bottom T gap; Chinook depth-refuge). **No new tagging.**

Physics: CTD on a diving animal; spatial bias toward prey.
