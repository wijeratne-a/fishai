---

## Species / class

Surface-schooling pelagics (sardine, anchovy, menhaden). Not oysters. Turbid Willapa is a **likely fail** for lidar depth.

## Mechanism

Airborne/UAV green polarized lidar + camera; coincident echosounder. PACE used only as **optical habitat prior** (`H-4.20`), never as school detector (~1.2 km pixels).

Physics: 532 nm scattering vs particles; kelp/wakes/bubbles confound ([Churnside et al. 2003](https://psl.noaa.gov/technology/instruments/floe/pdf/A_comparison_of_lidar_and_echosounder_measurements_of_fish.pdf); [NOAA ML ROI](https://repository.library.noaa.gov/view/noaa/41012/noaa_41012_DS1.pdf); [PACE TM](https://spo.nmfs.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/NMFS_TM_HyperspectralOC-SPO-TM-255_1.pdf)).

## Evidence

Airborne lidar **can** see schools in published Gulf of Mexico / California tests. Not operational global survey. UAV HS in ponds maps **chl**, not ocean schools.

## Requirements

Clear water, aircraft/UAV, lidar, EK80 vessel, weather window. Skip entirely if wedge is oyster WA.

## Coverage

Survey-day swaths, tens of meters depth max.

## Latency

Hours–days processing; ML ROI reduces but does not eliminate audit.

## Minimum viable experiment

Pre-register: lidar school detections vs EK80 presence along track (precision/recall). Camera must veto kelp/wakes. Public product: none (spots).

## Ground truth

EK80 + camera. Not PACE.

## FP / FN

Kelp, wakes, bubbles; deep/turbid FN. Satellite resolution failure.

## Legal / privacy / eco

School maps = spot maps (\(L\) high if published). Eye-safe protocols.

## Economics

ESTIMATE: \(5\times10^5\)–\(1\times10^6\) campaign.

## Kill criteria

1. Satellite pixels labeled schools.  
2. Kelp/wake FP unsolved.  
3. Public waypoints.  
4. Funded despite oyster-only wedge.

## Scale path

Only inside a pelagic survey program. Not FishAI v0.
