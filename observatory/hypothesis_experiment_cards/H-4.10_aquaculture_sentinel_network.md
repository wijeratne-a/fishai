## Species / class

Pacific oyster *Magallana gigas* (AphiaID 836033) farmed grow-out; optional sentinel diploid/triploid split. Not Olympia oyster mixed in. Pathogens/HAB cells are **split streams**, not the ops-stress label.

## Mechanism

In-bag or adjacent loggers (T, S, DO) + tide/emersion + forecast air T/heat index + waves → pre-registered 72 h rule. Growers log workability and protocol mortality. Optional SoundToxins/IFCB as **context**.

Physics: intertidal heat is **air × solar × emersion**, not 1 km SST (Raymond et al. 2022; Hesketh & Harley 2023 as used by FishAI red team).

## Evidence

- [UF/IFAS FARMS 2025–26](https://shellfish.ifas.ufl.edu/farms-2023/) — T/S/DO in bags, sentinel oysters, pathology.  
- [ULTFARMS Belwind](https://ultfarms.eu/pilot-6-belwind/) sensors + eDNA disease.  
- NANOOS Shellfish Growers; WA Sea Grant Rapid Response.  
- SoundToxins ≠ tissue toxin legality.

## Requirements

5–15 leases, DUA, culture-method metadata, DOH status shown **only as attributed official context**, not as \(y\). Human NSSP reviewer before any customer brief.

## Coverage

Named growing areas / leases internally; public ≥ growing-area. Willapa ≠ Hood Canal.

## Latency

Logger: minutes. Decision brief: daily. Pathology: days–weeks.

## Minimum viable experiment

As marine-domain E1 / red-team oyster test:

1. Pre-register 72 h rule: daylight emersion ∩ forecast air/heat ∩ waves.  
2. 30–60 days including a spring-tide heat window if season allows.  
3. Score workability + protocol mortality vs (a) persistence (b) SST-only.  
4. Success: ops indicator has lead time **and** users do not treat it as DOH.  
5. Failure of SST-only vs air+tide confirms the mechanism.

## Ground truth

Partner mortality/workability logs; PSI/WSG event notes. **Not** WA DOH closures.

## FP / FN

FP: heat without death (handling, ploidy, shade). FN: delayed mortality to day 30; hypoxia off-lease; OsHV-1 (not a WA 72 h default). Label contamination from Vp/HAB.
