## Species / class

## Mechanism

Fin/tissue from **already legally retained** fish → GT-seq / Pacific Rim SNP baseline → hierarchical GSI. Optional PBT if hatchery pedigrees available.

Physics: allele frequency differences; Mendelian parentage.

## Evidence

- [Van Doornik et al. 2024 NAFM](https://doi.org/10.1002/nafm.11019) — 389 pops; 97.8% tier-1, up to 97.7% tier-2 **in simulations/cross-validation**, not a FishAI number.  
- [Dryad SNP baseline](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.dz08kps5b)  
- [PBT+GSI BC marine fisheries](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8127719/)  
- [tGMR Chilkat](https://doi.org/10.1111/eva.13647) — abundance **in that design**, not ocean charter 48 h.
