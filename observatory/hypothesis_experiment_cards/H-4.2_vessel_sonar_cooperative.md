# Experiment card — H-4.2 Global vessel sonar cooperative

**hypothesis_id:** `H-4.2-SONAR-COOP`  
**experiment_id:** `EXP-SONAR-EDGE-01`  
**rank:** 10 of 15  
**date:** 2026-09-18  
**readiness:** `REQUIRES_PARTNERSHIP`  
**honest quantity:** calibrated NASC/Sv features, **not** species biomass, **not** a public fish-finder

---

## Species / class

Acoustic scatterers with gas bladders or strong TS (small pelagics, krill in polar fisheries). Siphonophores and bubbles are **confounders**, not targets. Do not run this as the v0 oyster product.

## Mechanism

Hull-mounted scientific or fishery echosounder (EK/ES60/80 class) → **on-board** processing (Echopype or Krillscan-class) → compact features: time, coarsened lat/lon, depth bins, NASC or mean Sv, quality flags → upload after **≥24 h delay**. Raw `.raw` echograms stay on the vessel or in a partner-only vault.

Physical principle: volume backscatter from impedance contrast. NASC ≠ kg without TS and mix.

## Evidence

- [ICES GAIN / WKGAIN](https://github.com/ices-eg/wk_WKGAIN) — processed water-column backscatter sharing, not raw secrets.
- [Haris et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-00785-8) IMOS processed acoustics.
- [Krillscan](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/383813725_KRILLSCAN_An_automated_open-source_software_for_processing_and_analysis_of_echosounder_data_from_the_Antarctic_krill_fishery) — 100× compression, near-real-time possible.
- Willingness to share is **conditional** ([Norwegian fleet 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.marpol.2025.106620)): anonymity, delay, reciprocity, access control.
- Federated learning is **not** demonstrated on echograms; do not claim it.

## Requirements

- 1–3 vessels with **calibrated** sounders; sphere calibration record; GPS/clock; edge PC; DUA forbidding public spot maps; human skipper liaison.
- Uncalibrated recreational units are **out**.

## Coverage

Along-track only. Public grain **≥10 km, ≥24 h**, rule-of-3 vessels before any multi-vessel product.

## Latency

Edge compute: faster than recording rate (Krillscan claim — **verify on the actual PC**). Cooperative product: delayed by contract (24–168 h). Science vault may be faster under DUA.

## Minimum viable experiment

1. Install edge processor on **one** vessel already doing a scientific or krill/pelagic trip.  
2. Blind comparison: edge NASC vs scientist-scrutinized NASC on the same files (pass if relative error within pre-registered band, e.g. 20% on EDSU means — **lock the number before looking**).  
3. Privacy test: can a harbormaster recover a fishing aggregation within 500 m from the public SKU? If yes, coarsen or kill public SKU.

## Ground truth

Scientific scrutinization (LSSS/Echoview), nets/optics for mix, calibration sphere.

## FP / FN

FP: aeration, bottom, uncalibrated gain, siphonophore resonance.  
FN: vessel avoidance, surface blind zone, fish below beam.

## Legal / privacy / eco

Highest L penalty in the top 10. No MMSI in public features. No combination with AIS to name highliners (FishAI privacy reverse-engineering test). Active acoustics at fishery levels only.

## Economics

ESTIMATE: \(5\times10^4\)–\(1.5\times10^5\) for one vessel-year software + QC + incentive. Do not buy a fleet until privacy test passes.

## Kill criteria

1. Leak or reverse-engineering failure.  
2. Uncalibrated sounders in the index.  
3. NASC labeled species abundance.  
4. Partners quit after season 1.

## Scale path

One vessel → GAIN-compatible netCDF → regional pelagic cooperative with delay/anonymity → **never** a consumer “bite map.”
