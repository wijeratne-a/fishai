## Species / class

American lobster / Dungeness / other pot fisheries if locked; otherwise any partner with buoys. Oyster farms may use loggers instead (`H-4.10`) — cheaper, no GNSS.

## Mechanism

Smart buoy (Farallon-class) records GPS, acceleration, SST; owner app. Science SKU: **hourly SST and soak flags** at coarsened cells, delay 24 h, rule-of-3. EM cameras are a separate optional module.

Physics: GNSS at surface; thermistor; IMU. Motion ≠ catch until validated.

## Requirements

DUA; owner dashboard first; no public map; optional pairing with eMOLT-class bottom T (better for lobster than SST).

## Coverage

Deployed gear only. Unfished cells remain unknown.

## Latency

Satellite minutes for the **owner**. Science SKU delayed.

## Minimum viable experiment

1. 10–30 buoys on one willing operator.  
2. SST vs independent logger (pass: RMSE within pre-registered °C).  
3. Privacy test: public SKU must fail 500 m recovery of strings.  
4. Do **not** build an entanglement classifier for users without a labeled trial.

## Ground truth

HOBO/eMOLT T; logbook soak; observer/EM subsample for catch — Category C, not N.

## FP / FN

IMU events as catch/entanglement; missed ghost gear; hyperstable CPUE if later modeled (Harley 2001 — cite, don’t ignore).

## Legal / privacy / eco

Highest L among cheap experiments. ALWTRP / MSA confidentiality analogue. Ropeless may **lower** \(E\) if it replaces vertical lines — not automatic.

2. Density = abundance.  
3. Unvalidated entanglement alerts.  
4. Operator withdrawal after leak.
