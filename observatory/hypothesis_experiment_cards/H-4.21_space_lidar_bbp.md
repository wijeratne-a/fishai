## Species / class

Particles and phytoplankton. Krill-from-CALIOP literature is **weak, not adopted**.

## Mechanism

CALIOP 532 nm archive bbp, ICESat-2 ATLAS subsurface photons, optional EarthCARE ATLID 355 nm. Instrument artifacts (crosstalk, afterpulsing) must be the published corrections, not a new NN on CPUE.

## Evidence

[Lu et al. 2021](https://doi.org/10.1029/2021ea001839); [CALIOP bbp v1](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/catalog/ob-cloud-calipso-caliop-l3-bbp-1); [ATLID abstract](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-egu26-10155). CALIOP ended July 2023; archive remains.

## Requirements / coverage / latency

NASA public; orbit curtains; first optical depths; night/high-lat advantage over passive color.

## Minimum viable experiment

Hold-out BGC-Argo bbp/chl in one basin. Do not score vs fish landings.

## Ground truth

BGC-Argo, HPLC. Not CPUE.

## FP / FN

Uncorrected artifacts; “lidar saw the fish.”

## Legal / privacy / eco

Negligible.

## Economics

Folded into \(2\times10^4\)–\(5\times10^4\) PACE matchup.

## Kill criteria

1. bbp as fish/krill \(N\) without acoustic/net truth.  
2. Uncorrected artifacts.  
3. High confidence in unmatchable coastal Case-2 water.

## Scale path

Night/polar complement to `H-4.20`. Not a nekton survey.
