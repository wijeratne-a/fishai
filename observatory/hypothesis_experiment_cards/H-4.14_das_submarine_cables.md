**killed variant:** DAS as general fish census  
**honest quantity:** low-frequency baleen-call detection/localization along a cable  
**forbidden quantity:** fish \(N\); public whale tracks

---

## Species / class

Fin, blue, pygmy blue, Omura’s and other mysticetes with energy ≲100 Hz. Most fishes unsupported.

## Mechanism

Interrogator on dark fiber → Rayleigh-backscatter strain → template/FK/YOLO detections → localization (one or two cables).

Physics: acoustic pressure → fiber strain; SNR vs range and seafloor coupling ([Svalbard two-cable](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2023.1130898); [Oregon 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2025.1603541/full); [NW Australia](https://doi.org/10.1071/ep23268); [JASA 2025 vs hydrophones](https://doi.org/10.1121/10.0037512)).

## Evidence

Published detections and ~100 m localization in dedicated trials. **Not** operational NOAA product.

## Requirements

Cable owner + landing-state security + hydrophone audit + data volume plan. Unlikely in remaining 2026 without an existing relationship.

## Coverage

Tens–hundreds of km along that cable only.

## Latency

Research networks have shown near-real-time streams; ops is unproven.

## Minimum viable experiment

Precision/recall vs hydrophone array on a **pre-registered** call class. Privacy: tracks NEVER_PUBLISH; at most delayed presence in a large box for managers.

## Ground truth

Hydrophones; AIS for vessels (noise, not fish); airgun localization where ethical/legal.

## FP / FN

Ships, quakes, coupling gaps; missed high-frequency taxa.

## Legal / privacy / eco

High \(L\) (MMPA, security). Passive \(E\) low.

## Economics

ESTIMATE: \(10^6\)–\(10^7\) / cable-year.

## Kill criteria

1. Marketed as fish.  
2. Public tracks.  
3. P/R below pre-registered vs hydrophones.  
4. No cable access after 90 days of outreach.

## Scale path

Manager-only strike-mitigation along cables. Not FishAI v0.
