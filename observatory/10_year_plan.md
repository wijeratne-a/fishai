# Ten-year planetary observation vision — scientifically honest

**Horizon:** 2026-09-18 → 2036-09-18  
**Genre:** vision with **hard cannots**. Not a pitch deck.

The observatory’s 10-year success is **not** that every saltwater organism is tracked. It is that the world has a **continuously updated, evidence-typed, privacy-aware fabric** in which:

- empty ocean is shown as empty of evidence,  
- a few cells are genuinely operational,  
- new sensing is aimed at residual uncertainty,  
- and no one mistakes a model for a fish.

---

## 1. Vision (what to build toward)

A **living digital twin** in the original sense of the program:

> prior ecology + historical observations + current environmental state + new observations + ocean physics + movement biology + uncertainty  
> → posterior **where the posterior is allowed to be UNKNOWN**.

Planetary **observation** means:

1. **Catalog completeness** — most named marine taxa have a T0–T2 card (taxonomy, range sketch, suitability *if* data, known gaps).  
2. **Coverage maps that are useful** — latency, depth, effort, rights, ecological sensitivity.  
3. **Operational twins** in **tens of cells**, not tens of thousands of species.  
4. **Fusion where surveys already go** — acoustics × eDNA × catch × physics on the same lines.  
5. **Opportunistic networks** — farms, ferries, fishing gear, cables — with **edge features and coarsening**.  
6. **Public-good backbone** still owned by IOOS/GOOS/Copernicus/navies/universities; this project **assimilates and explains**, it does not replace NOAA.

## 2. What should exist in 2036 if this works

| Layer | 2036 picture |
| --- | --- |
| UI | Evidence-class globe of **coverage/unknown**; drill-in to cell briefs; no count theater |
| Taxonomy | WoRMS-aligned graph; life stages; stocks as first-class where they exist |
| Physics | Global models as **priors** (CMEMS-class), locally corrected by in situ |
| Biology | T6 in a **minority** of socioeconomically or conservation-critical cells |
| Sensing | Partner sonar cooperatives in some fleets; eDNA **campaigns and some autonomous nodes**, not a homogeneous mesh; PAM/DAS on **some** cables for vocal taxa; AUVs **tasked by uncertainty**, not orbiting every grid cell |
| Governance | Publish classes enforced; Indigenous data sovereignty; NEVER_PUBLISH for breeding/nesting/listed fine-scale |
| Commercial FishAI | Possibly several sequential wedges over a decade, **each** still launched as 1×1×1×1 |

**Spend class (ESTIMATE):** a startup/research org **$10–50M over 10 years** for software+partnerships+experiments. A true planetary sensor program is **$100M–$1B+** and is a **consortium/government** object (see cost model). Do not conflate them.

## 3. What global coverage **still cannot mean in 10 years**

Restated because this is the load-bearing sentence of the decade:

**Even in 2036, “global coverage” cannot mean a real-time census or tracker of saltwater life.**

It cannot mean:

1. Exact location of every (or most) individual fish, invertebrate, microbe, or larva.  
2. Exact abundance or biomass in unsurveyed cells.  
3. Real-time tracking of untagged populations.  
4. Direct biological abundance from satellite SST, ocean color, or vessel density/AIS.  
5. Uniform, depth-resolved biomass of the global ocean.  
6. Species-resolved identity of all acoustic targets.  
7. eDNA detections as GPS coordinates of organisms (transport, decay, contamination).  
8. DAS as a complete marine-life observatory (vocal / geophysical ≠ nekton census).  
9. Operational T6 forecasts for most taxa×places (most remain T0–T2).  
10. Food-safety, legal harvest, navigation, or weather-safety as twin outputs.  
11. Public fine-scale maps of ESA/MMPA listed animals, spawning aggregations, nests, or private fishing/farm performance.  
12. Closure of the unknown — **UNKNOWN remains a high-quality, first-class output.**  
13. A foundation model that is *correctly confident* everywhere (the failure mode is confidence in voids).  
14. Knowledge of the deep sea, polar night, and highly turbid estuaries comparable to a well-instrumented temperate estuary.  
15. Ethical tagging of “millions of organisms” as a solved logistics/welfare problem.

These are not pessimism. They are **physics, sampling theory, welfare, and law**.

---

## 4. Decade sensing thesis (without skipping hardware-last)

Years 1–3: **software + partners + existing sensors.**  
Years 4–7: **opportunistic networks** (gear, farms, ferries) and **targeted** eDNA/PAM experiments.  
Years 7–10: **infrastructure plays only with others paying** — cable DAS science, IOOS expansions, autonomous molecular nodes at ports.

If Year 1–3 never produce one operational cell, **do not** jump to Year 7 toys.

## 5. Taxa in 2036 (vision, not a promise)

| Will be better observed | Will still be mostly unknown |
| --- | --- |
| Farmed bivalves in instrumented estuaries | Most wild benthos |
| A few assessed fish stocks at **seasonal** grain | 24h school-scale most species |
| Vocal marine mammals along some cables/PAM | Silent / deep odontocetes uniformly |
| Surface blooms | Subsurface thin layers globally |
| Tagged subsets of charisma species | Untagged juveniles of most fishes |
| Microbial **campaigns** at stations | Continuous global microbiome |

## 6. UI in 2036

The winning interface is still not a video-game ocean. It is:

- **Unknown-first globe** (hatch, latency, effort).  
- **Cell brief** with evidence class, depth, time, provenance, what-it-is-not.  
- **Individual tracks** (T5) visually **quarantined** from population layers.  
- **Research mode** banner on hypotheses.

If the 2036 demo looks like a fish GPS, the project failed.

---

## 7. Relation to commercial FishAI in 2036

The company (if it exists) may have sequenced several wedges: oyster ops → maybe lobster CPUE → maybe another aquaculture species. Each wedge is a **cell of the observatory**. The observatory is the **scientific memory and architecture**. It is not a license to ship “all species” to paying users.

---

## 8. Success definition for the decade

**Success:** one can query any named marine taxon and receive an **honest tier**, an **unknown map**, and — only where earned — a calibrated forecast; governments and partners trust the publish classes; at least one commercial decision loop still exists without claim inflation.

**Failure:** a beautiful globe that interpolates life into voids, leaks sensitive locations, or sells SST as fish.
