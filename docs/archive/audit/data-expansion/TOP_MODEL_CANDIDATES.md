# Top model candidates

Ranks stay inside one region and one method.

## Florida Keys visual census

1. *Chaetodon capistratus* is already fit internally. It is the current baseline.
2. *Sparisoma viride* and *Haemulon flavolineatum* are the fallbacks from the same three years.
3. *Epinephelus itajara* is in the species list and is not a model candidate. Eight detection events across three years.

## Other Atlantic visual-census frames

Puerto Rico 2023, USVI 2023, and Flower Garden Banks 2024 each repeat one species list and store zeros. A common species on each frame can take the same spatial-block baseline. They are not pooled with Florida, because the species lists and sample sizes differ. Flower Garden Banks has 38 events in 2024, so a species model there needs a reviewer to judge fold support.

## Pacific visual counts

Hawaii, American Samoa, CNMI/Guam, and the Pacific Remote Islands returned positive counts and no zeros. They can show where a survey recorded a species. They cannot support a detection model until a zero rule is demonstrated.

## Recent occurrence reports

The OBIS Keys extract is a freshness layer. Its newest saved event date is 2026-07-20. That is not a map of where fish are on 2026-09-24.
