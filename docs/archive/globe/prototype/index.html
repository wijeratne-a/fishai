    Learn & Methods

    Measured means someone recorded a value (for example water temperature).

    Estimate means a model inferred something when a published card allows it. It is not a sighting. No estimate is issued in this build.

    Forecast means a published prediction for a named time window. It is not live tracking. No forecast is issued in this build.

    Past reports = where people recorded this species in the past, not live presence.

    Unknown (stripes, where a layer is drawn) means we do not have an estimate. It does not mean the ocean is empty. Unknown is the default scientific status.

    Observed presence, occurrence probability, relative abundance, movement, habitat suitability, and unknown stay separate. Habitat is favorable conditions — not confirmed presence. Current estimates and forecasts stay off unless a model card is actually published.

    Searching yellowfin tuna, Atlantic goliath grouper, or most names should say we have no current location and no forecast. Goliath aggregation sites stay coarsened or withheld. Past reports from public databases are a historical pattern, not where the animals are now. Sensitive taxa (for example white shark) withhold locations.
