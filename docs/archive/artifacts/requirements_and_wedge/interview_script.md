# Interview script — FishAI wedge validation

**Purpose:** Test whether a **named** recurring decision is real, measurable, and worth a brief — not to sell a dashboard.  
**Status:** Do not contact operators until the founder locks a wedge and authorizes outreach.  
**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Length:** 30–40 minutes  
**Recording:** Only with consent. No exact farm coordinates, trap/set GPS, or Indigenous knowledge in notes. Coarsen locations to growing area / salmon management area / statistical area.

**30-day gate (master prompt):** 15 structured interviews. Prioritize the locked wedge (8–12) plus 3–5 adjacent experts (Sea Grant, PSI, ODFW/CDFW field, DMR, PCSGA) who are **not** sold the product.

---

## Consent (read aloud)

> This is research for a possible operational brief, not a government survey. You can skip any question. We will not publish your name, farm/boat identity, or any exact locations. We will not ask you to share data today. This is not fishing, harvest, or food-safety advice.

If they decline: thank them; log “declined” only.

---

## Screener (2 min)

1. Role and years in it.
2. Species you actually handle most days (Pacific oyster / Chinook charter / lobster traps / other — **stop if other and the founder locked a species**).
3. Geography: growing area name / port and ocean salmon area / lobster zone or statistical area if known.
4. Are you the person who decides tomorrow’s crew, trip, or haul plan? If not, who is?

**Disqualify from “customer” count:** regulators acting as regulators, NGOs without an operating decision, people outside the locked geography.

---

## Block A — The decision (10 min)

Use **only the locked wedge** module.

### If W1 — oyster farm

5. Walk me through yesterday’s (or last spring tide’s) crew decision. What did you look at, in order?
6. In the last two summers, what forced you to **not** work a tide you had planned? (heat, wind, DO, bloom, staffing, DOH closure — keep them separate)
7. When oysters are already stressed, do you change **handling** (yes/no/example)?
8. How many hours ahead is a useful heads-up? What is too late?
9. If you got one message at 5 a.m., what sentence would change the day?
10. What must that message **never** say (especially about harvest legality or “safe to eat”)?

### If W2 — Chinook charter

5. Last Chinook open day: how did you pick the area? What would have changed your mind at 5 a.m.?
6. How do you define a “good” vs “zero” Chinook trip (kept + released, clients happy, $)?
7. Fuel and time: what is a wasted steam worth, roughly?
8. After 2023–24 CA closures (if relevant): what do you target instead, and would a Chinook brief still matter in 2026?
9. Would you ever use a public map of productive cells? Why/why not?
10. What official sources do you already check (ODFW/CDFW, PFMC, NWS)?

### If W3 — lobster

5. Last trip: how did you decide which strings to haul?
6. What does a “slow” vs “good” haul mean in legal count per trap?
7. How much of the decision is molt/temperature vs just where your gear already is?
8. Whale-gear and area rules: what would a third-party tool have to do to **not** get in the way?
9. Would you share coarsened haul counts if locations stayed private and delayed?
10. What would make you refuse?

---

## Block B — Current tools and substitutes (5 min)

11. List every tool opened last week for this decision (tides, NANOOS, NWS, group texts, buyer, association).
12. What is missing from those tools **for this decision**?
13. Have you paid for any ocean or farm software? What, and did you keep it?

---

## Block C — Measurable outcome (5 min)

14. Could you report, in 30 seconds after the tide/trip:
    - W1: worked Y/N; unusual mortality Y/N; optional % if counted
    - W2: coarsened area; angler-hours; Chinook 0 / some / good (their words, then quantify)
    - W3: coarsened cell; trap-hauls; legal count
15. Would you do that daily for 30 days if the brief was free? If not, why?
16. Historical logs already on paper/phone? Rights to use them?

---

## Block D — Economics without asking “what will you pay?” first (5 min)

17. Cost of a wasted crew-day / trip (labor + fuel + bait), even as a range or “more than X.”
18. Worst loss event in the last five years that a 48–72h heads-up **might** have changed. What was the $ or % if they volunteer it.
19. Who pays for tools — owner, skipper, co-op, buyer?

**Pricing (only if Block A showed a real decision):**

20. Would you try a 90-day paid pilot at $X/month? **Do not lead with a number until the founder sets a hypothesis.** If they volunteer a number, record it as anecdote (Tier 4) until repeated.

---

## Block E — Trust, privacy, tribes, regulation (5 min)

21. Any data that must never leave the boat/farm?
22. Tribal harvest, usual-and-accustomed areas, or co-management — anything we must not model or map?
23. Who is the official source you trust for closures / seasons / whales / biotoxins?

---

## Block F — Design partner close (2 min)

24. Willing to receive a **manual** brief (human-assembled, sources cited) for two weeks?
25. Introductions to two peers in the **same** locked geography?
26. Best contact method.

## Expert add-on (WSG, PSI, agency field staff)

- What do operators **actually** change after a heat, hypoxia, or slow CPUE week?
- Which public datasets do they already ignore because of latency or location mismatch?
- Where would a startup cause harm (spots, leases, tribal data, food-safety confusion)?

---

## Logging standard

Store: date, wedge ID, role, coarsened geography, scores, quotes **without** identifiers. Privacy tier: RESTRICTED. Do not put names in git.
