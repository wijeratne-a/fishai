# Pilot Offer — FishAI Oyster Ops-Risk Brief

**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Audience:** Washington Pacific oyster farm owner or farm manager  
**Status:** Draft commercial offer. **Not signed. Not evidence of a customer.** Legal terms require HUMAN LEGAL REVIEW. **Not a model score.** After founder lock this would still be a **manual** Category D ops-stress / work-window brief: not harvest, not food-safety, not SST-as-body-temperature.

---

## One-sentence offer

For 8 weeks we will email you a **72-hour operational stress brief** for one primary lease (and up to two more) by 16:30 local, every operating day — so you can decide whether to **monitor, delay handling, lash gear, or reassign crew**. It is **not** a harvest, food-safety, or closure product. In return you spend **30 seconds** after work windows telling us what actually happened.

---

## Who this is for

- You grow Pacific oysters in a named Washington growing area.
- You already look at weather, tides, and NANOOS (or you skip them and go by feel).
- You make a **crew / boat / handling / gear** plan on a 1–3 day horizon.
- You can name **one lease** that hurts when heat, waves, low oxygen, or freshwater surprises you.

## Who this is not for

- Anyone wanting us to say whether product is **safe to harvest or eat**
- Recreational harvest advice
- A replacement for WDOH, the Vp harvest plan, or your HACCP
- A public map of your beds
- A new mobile app you must train the crew to use

1. 30-minute kickoff: leases, culture method, your existing “don’t work if…” rules, contacts.
2. Signed **pilot + data-use** (drafted; counsel review) — farm data **PRIVATE**.
3. Outcome form after each work window you actually attempted or skipped because of conditions (**target: 60%+ of work days**).

## Disclaimers (will appear on every brief)

This product is an **environmental / operational stress / work-window indicator** (Category D). It is **not**:

- a food-safety determination
- harvest authorization or a closure notice
- a substitute for Washington State Department of Health growing-area classification, commercial closures, or your Vibrio control plan (WAC 246-282-006)
- a weather-safety, navigation, or insurance product
- a guarantee of mortality, survival, growth, or marketability
- satellite or station water temperature as oyster **body temperature**
- a combined ops + sanitation score

If WDOH or your own observations conflict with a brief, **they win**.

## Success — how we both know it worked

At week 8 we look **only** at:

1. Did you **open** most briefs?
2. Did you **log outcomes** ≥60% of work days?
3. Did you **do something different** at least twice (even “ignored it and that was correct”)?
4. Would you **pay** $750–$1,500 for 90 more days — yes / no / not at that price?
5. Would you **intro** one peer?

No fake testimonials. If the answer is no, we stop or redesign.
