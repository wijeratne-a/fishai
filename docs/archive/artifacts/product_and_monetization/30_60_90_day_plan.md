### Interview script (30 min) — do not lead

1. Walk me through planning **tomorrow’s** work (tools, times, people).
2. Last time conditions burned you (heat, waves, DO, freshwater, crew wasted) — what happened, $ or hours?
3. What do you open **every day** (NANOOS, WDOH, weather, group text)?
4. If an email at 16:30 said Elevated/Typical/High for **one lease**, with “not food-safety,” would you read it?
5. What would make you **delete** it (wrong harvest advice, crying wolf, stale)?
6. Would you tap a 30s form after work? Why not?
7. Price: too cheap / stretch / too expensive for daily May–Oct ($400 / $900 / $1,500 / season pack).
8. Who else decides budget?
9. Intro to one peer? (ask last)

Record: workflow, substitutes, WTP band, food-safety confusion, log willingness. **No** quoting them in marketing without a release.

## Days 61–90 — spec 90-day gate (need **one**)

Master spec: at least one of:

1. Paid **recurring** pilot  
2. Paid report/site/season analysis  
3. Measured forecast **lift** over baseline  
4. A **real customer action** caused by the product  
5. Repeat outcome reporting  
6. Permissioned data-sharing agreement that can create a proprietary loop  

**Plan to hit (1) or (4) + (5)** — lift (3) may not be statistically pretty by day 90 with n=3 farms; do not fake AUROC.

| If we have | Then |
|---|---|
| Paid 90-day + logs ≥70% | Automate **source pull + PDF render**; keep human QA on Elevated/High |
| Logs but no pay | Price/job failed → pivot_memo |
| Pay but no logs | Product is a newsletter; kill flywheel or change incentives |
| Food-safety bleed incident | Stop sending; red team; maybe stop wedge |

**Still forbidden at day 90 without evidence:** terminal UI, second species, hardware, public maps, AIS lobster.
