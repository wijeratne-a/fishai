## Template 1 — R1, NCEI 0282183 and 0306184

**To:** Jeremiah Blondeau (address printed on the NCEI Florida NCRMP fish-community landing pages)  
**Copy:** ncei.info@noaa.gov  
**Subject:** Written yes or no for research use of NCEI 0282183 and 0306184

Hello,

I am writing about two public NCEI packages of National Coral Reef Monitoring Program fish surveys on the Florida Reef Tract:

- Accession 0282183, survey dates 1 June 2022 through 28 November 2022
- Accession 0306184, survey dates 29 May 2024 through 26 November 2024

Parent collection: https://doi.org/10.7289/v52n50ks

We are not asking for site coordinates, wrecks, spawning locations, or fishing information. We need a written yes or no on whether we may:

1. download the packages and store them outside a public code repository;
2. keep native coordinates in that protected store only;
3. train a detection model on completed dives;
4. show only coarse probabilities, with raw coordinates withheld;
5. cite the accessions and the parent DOI as you specify.

Please also answer:

- Are absences stored as zeros, or only as positive species rows that can be joined to a complete dive list?
- Which columns are the dive id, date, depth, effort, habitat or stratum, and species code?
- Does the 2024 single-stage design match the 2022 two-stage design closely enough for a time comparison?
- May a coarse public map be shown for a common reef fish? We will not request a goliath grouper map unless you say a coarse product is acceptable.
- Who on the survey team can review a methods note before anything is published?

A yes, a no, or a pointer to the governing terms is enough. I will follow up on 2 October 2026 if I have not heard back.

Thank you.

## Template 2 — R2, CalCOFI CUFES and WCOFS

**To:** the contact published by the CalCOFI program  
**Copy:** the contact published for the West Coast Operational Forecast System  
**Subject:** Written yes or no for research use of CUFES egg counts and WCOFS fields

Hello,

We are asking whether we may use Continuous Underway Fish Egg Sampler counts for Pacific sardine and northern anchovy, together with matching West Coast Operational Forecast System surface fields, for an internal detection or egg-presence prototype.

We need a written yes or no on acquisition, protected storage, model training, and display of coarse probabilities only. We will not redistribute raw cruise tracks.

Please also say whether a completed sampler segment with a zero egg count is present in the public tables, which identifier and date fields to use, and which citation you require.

We are not asking for fishing locations. I will follow up on 2 October 2026 if I have not heard back.

Thank you.
