## Species / class

Phytoplankton / BGC tracers only. Animals enter later as **users** of habitat fields, not as twin state.

## Mechanism

Consume Copernicus Marine / EDITO analyses and forecasts. Score against held-out BGC-Argo and (if licensed) gliders. Optional: compare persistence and satellite-only chl.

Physical principle: primitive equations + BGC ODEs + variational/ensemble DA ([MedBFM NN+DA](https://os.copernicus.org/articles/20/689/2024/); [SEAMLESS ensemble](https://os.copernicus.org/articles/20/155/2024/os-20-155-2024.pdf)).

## Requirements

- CMEMS account; product IDs locked; as-of timestamps (no leakage).  
- Independent BGC-Argo list **not** used in the scored product if possible; otherwise document overlap.  
- License check ([EMODnet ToU](https://emodnet.ec.europa.eu/en/terms-use-emodnet-online-services-data-and-data-products)).

## Coverage

Global/regional model grids. Useful FishAI grain still lease/port/SA — twin does not magically refine biology.

## Latency

Operational oceanography: hours–days. Fine for 24–72 h **environmental** priors.

## Minimum viable experiment

1. Pick one region overlapping a candidate wedge (NE Pacific shelf or GOM).  
2. Pre-register RMSE/bias and 80% ensemble coverage for chl and O2 vs hold-out profiles for 90 days.  
3. If coverage outside 0.70–0.90, print `uncertainty_rationale` only — no fake ±.  
4. **Stop** if anyone labels the twin “oyster health” or “lobster abundance.”

## Ground truth

BGC-Argo, NANOOS/NERRS in situ (oyster geography), eMOLT-class bottom T (lobster) as **physical** GT, not animal GT.

## FP / FN

Overconfident coastal chl; NN-reconstructed nutrients treated as data; missing hypoxia in unresolved fjords (Hood Canal).

## Legal / privacy / eco

Low. Do not assimilate VMS/logbooks.

## Economics

ESTIMATE: \(10^4\)–\(3\times10^4\) analyst-months. Nested custom twin: \(10^7\)+ **not** this experiment.

## Kill criteria

1. Twin emits animal \(N\).  
2. Ensemble reliability fails and is still shown as High.  
3. SST-only beats the twin on a **biological** label — then this twin is not the mechanism for that label (may still be useful for other labels).

## Scale path

Scored CMEMS priors → oyster air×tide+DO brief → larval OpenDrift forcing (`H-4.5`). Do not build a fish DA until biological observing density exists.
