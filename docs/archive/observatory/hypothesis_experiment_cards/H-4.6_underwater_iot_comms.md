## Species / class

None. This experiment measures **the channel and the node**, so that biological sensors (`H-4.7`, `H-4.10`, `H-4.19`) stay up.

## Mechanism

On an **existing** partner mooring (farm, NANOOS, HABON): log biofouling on optical window and power budget; optional short acoustic hop to a surface gateway. Do **not** design an EM mesh.

Physics: EM skin depth in seawater kills MHz–GHz range; acoustics: km, low bit-rate; optics: high rate, turbidity-limited ([2025 review](https://doi.org/10.54254/2755-2721/2025.22225)). Fouling is the field failure mode ([wave-UV SBIR](https://sbir.org/awards/doe-DE-SC0020921-3)).

## Evidence

Lab optical power transfer (ISCAS 2025) is **not** a year-long farm node. UWSN papers often simulate. Treat simulations as TRL 2–3.

## Requirements

Only run if a mooring already exists (piggyback). New custom modem R&D is out of FishAI scope.

## Coverage

One node. Not geographic coverage of life.

## Latency

Whatever the biological sensor needs; comms should not be the story.

## Minimum viable experiment

1. Photograph/measure optical-window transmission weekly for 90 days with/without existing wiper or UV if already installed.  
2. Pre-register kill: if unattended optical SNR for IFCB/fluorometer drops below usable in <30 days without a proven cleaner, **do not** propose optical IoUT in that water mass.  
3. Document energy: battery vs harvest **measured**, not brochure.

## Ground truth

Transmission, current draw, packet logs. Not animals.

## FP / FN

Data gaps misread as biological zeros — flag `missing_data_state`.

## Legal / privacy / eco

Acoustic duty cycle vs marine mammals: consult if adding pings. No TBT. Batteries recovered.

## Economics

ESTIMATE: \(10^4\)–\(3\times10^4\) piggyback. Full IoUT: \(10^7\)+ **kill for this project year**.

## Kill criteria

1. Architecture uses long-range EM.  
2. Node <30 days survival.  
3. New acoustic noise without PAM review.  
4. IoUT promised as the observatory itself.

## Scale path

None until a **biological** MVE needs it. Prefer cabled/farm power (`H-4.10`) and ships (`H-4.16`) over wireless mesh.
