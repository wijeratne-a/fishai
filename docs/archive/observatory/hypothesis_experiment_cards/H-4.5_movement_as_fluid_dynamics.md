## Species / class

First target: **bivalve larvae** (Pacific oyster veligers) or Dungeness/cancer zoeae in a named estuary — matches sessile/coastal biology. Alternate: scyphomedusae with published depth preference. Not adult salmon or lobster walking.

## Mechanism

Assimilated 3D currents (CMEMS or regional ROMS) → OpenDrift / LTRANS particles with locked PLD, vertical behavior table, and river forcing → connectivity kernel vs genetic/otolith/drifter labels.

Physical principle: advection–diffusion of nearly passive particles; behavior is a **few parameters**.
