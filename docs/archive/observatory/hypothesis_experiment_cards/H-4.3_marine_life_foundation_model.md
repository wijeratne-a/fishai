## Species / class

Whatever FathomNet / TreeOfLife-200M / MarineInst20M actually contain (ROV megafauna, some fishes, long-tailed invertebrates). Not a designed survey of *Magallana*, Chinook, or *Homarus*.

## Mechanism

Run **open weights** (BioCLIP 2, MarineInst) as frozen encoders. Train only a small calibrated head if licenses allow; otherwise zero-shot classification. Primary test is **not accuracy** — it is whether confidence **falls** on geographic and taxonomic shifts.

This is not a sensor. There is no physical measurement of unseen water.

## Evidence

- [MarineInst](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/papers/00223.pdf) — marine instance masks/captions; still vision, not SDMs.
- [BioCLIP 2](https://imageomics.github.io/bioclip-2/) — 214M images including FathomNet; FishNet habitat/trait tasks are **image** tasks.
- [NicheFlow](https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.10.15.618541) — reptile SDM preprint; **do not** cite as marine abundance skill.

## Requirements

- License review of weights and eval images (DATA_RIGHTS).  
- Spatial blocking (not i.i.d. splits).  
- FishAI uncertainty fields: `confidence_category`, `observation_density`, `extrapolation_flag`.  
- **No training on private GPS or listed-species holding sites.**  
- Project gates still forbid advanced ML for the **product**; this experiment is an **evaluation notebook**, not a customer model.

## Coverage

Coverage = label coverage. Empty ocean remains empty.

## Latency

Offline. Irrelevant to 24–72 h ops unless someone illegally ships a heatmap.

## Minimum viable experiment

1. Pre-register: on a **held-out ocean basin or cruise**, the model must assign Low / abstain to ≥ pre-registered fraction of cells with zero local labels; High confidence precision must exceed Low.  
2. If High rate >95% or High is not more accurate than Low → **fail** (matches `uncertainty_policy.md` §7).  
3. Do **not** decode embeddings into a global species map.

## Ground truth

Expert FathomNet labels; independent cruises. Not landings.

## FP / FN

FP: confident wrong species; fluent empty-ocean maps.  
FN: rare taxa.  
**Catastrophic FP:** UI that fills sparse cells.

## Legal / privacy / eco

Open weights can memorize captions with locations — strip. No ESA pins. Ecological harm is **via effort misallocation**, not the GPU.

## Economics

## Kill criteria

1. Any High confidence without local labels.  
2. ECE worse than climatology on spatial blocks.  
3. Marketing “AI knows where ocean life is.”  
4. Training on disallowed location data.

## Scale path
