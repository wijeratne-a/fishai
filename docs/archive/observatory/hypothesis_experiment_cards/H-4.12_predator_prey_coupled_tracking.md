# Experiment card — H-4.12 Predator–prey coupled tracking

**hypothesis_id:** `H-4.12-PREDPREY`  
**experiment_id:** `EXP-PRED-ELECTIVITY-01`  
**rank:** not in top 15 as new tagging; desktop electivity is a low-cost **science** add-on  
**date:** 2026-09-18  
**readiness (desktop electivity):** `IMPLEMENTABLE_NOW` on published datasets  
**readiness (coupled tagging observatory):** `REQUIRES_NEW_SENSOR_DEPLOYMENT`  
**killed as 2026 product:** planetary food-web telemetry  
**honest quantity:** trait-based prey **selection** vs survey availability  
**forbidden quantity:** predator tracks as public forage maps

---

## Species / class

Literature-first: albacore / other tunas with diet + trawl time series; seabird–forage–subsurface predator triads. Do not start with ESA salmon or NARW.

## Mechanism

**No new tags in MVE.** Recompute electivity from published diet vs survey tables (e.g. [albacore CCLME traits](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecolind.2023.111473)). Coupled biologging ([bluefin–orca 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-80744-0); [shearwater–mahi–anchovy](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsbl.2024.0223)) is **evidence that coupling is real**, not a deployment plan.

## Evidence

As above. Coupled n is small; do not over-generalize n=1 ingestions.

## Requirements

Paper tables or DRYAD; licenses for reuse; no GPS of living listed predators.

## Coverage

Wherever the papers sampled. Not global.

## Latency

Offline.

## Minimum viable experiment

1. Reproduce one electivity result independently.  
2. Pre-register: does a traits prior beat taxonomy-only in a held-out year?  
3. If yes, traits may later inform a **forage habitat prior** — still not tuna N.  
4. New tagging only after permits and if a wedge needs it (unlikely for oyster 72 h).

## Ground truth

Stomachs vs trawls as in the source papers. Trawls ≠ what predators see (the papers already say this).

## FP / FN

Circular habitat inference; tag effects; diet climatology beating any “AI coupling.”

## Legal / privacy / eco

New tagging: high \(E\). Desktop: low. Never publish listed predator–prey pins.

## Economics

ESTIMATE: \(10^4\) desktop. Coupled cruise: \(10^6\)–\(10^7\).

## Kill criteria

1. Tracks as public forage maps.  
2. Tagging without permits.  
3. n too small to beat diet climatology.  
4. Proposed as FishAI v0.

## Scale path

Stay at synthesis until a funded ecological program exists. Observatory may **cite** coupling, not operate it.
