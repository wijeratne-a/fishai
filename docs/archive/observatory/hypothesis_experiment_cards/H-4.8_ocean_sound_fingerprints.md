## Species / class

Soniferous fishes, snapping shrimp, baleen and odontocetes **where detectors are validated**. The fingerprint of a site includes wind/vessels (not biology).

## Mechanism

Use NCEI / SanctSound / SoundCoop **hybrid millidecade** products plus daily aggregated detections. Do not re-detect on raw audio in v0.

Physics: pressure spectral density; masking; spreading.

## Requirements

DATA_RIGHTS pass on NCEI/OBIS reuse; calibration metadata; **no** fine mammal localization in any FishAI UI.

## Coverage

Existing sanctuaries and Noise Reference Stations — not global, not GOM lobster bottom, not Willapa oyster bags.

## Latency

Archive products: months–years lag common. Some dashboards faster. Label freshness honestly.

## Minimum viable experiment

1. Download HMD + daily detections for **one** sanctuary overlapping a future geography (Olympic Coast if oyster/Chinook west; Stellwagen if lobster).  
2. Pre-register: can HMD bands distinguish vessel-dominated vs biology-dominated nights better than a time-of-day climatology (simple classifier, blocked CV)?  
3. If used only as **context** (“noisy/quiet”) that is enough; do not predict CPUE from SPL.

## Ground truth

Manual spectrogram audit subsample; AIS for ships (effort, **not** fish); official mammal detections as presence only.

## FP / FN

Rain/vessels as fish choruses; missed calls in noise; energy ≠ biomass.
