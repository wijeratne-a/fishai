---

## Species / class

Luminescent dinoflagellates and zooplankton; mesopelagic micronekton only if a glider actually samples them. Not oysters (non-luminescent adults).

## Mechanism

Bathyphotometer or compact photon counter (Biosealight-class) on CTD or glider with mechanical stimulation; classify flash kinetics; pair with nets/IFCB.

Physics: ~450 nm luciferin–luciferase emission; photons are not chlorophyll fluorescence.

## Evidence

- [BIOLUMOPS 2024–2026](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-egu24-8144) glider + ship + (separately) ocean-color dinoflagellates.  
- [Biosealight OCEANS 2025](https://doi.org/10.1109/oceans58557.2025.11104671).  
- Historical Atlantic maps (Piontkovski) are **sparse expeditions**, not a mesh.

## Requirements

Dark profiles; stimulation protocol; IFCB or microscopy; do not use PACE as the bioluminescence sensor.

## Coverage

One glider-week in a known luminescent region (not first choice for Willapa turbidity).

## Latency

On-board counts: seconds. Classified ecology: days–weeks.

## Minimum viable experiment

Only if a partner already has a glider/photometer. Pre-register: flash-rate ranks dinoflagellate or zooplankton bins from IFCB/nets better than chl-a alone. If ocean color wins for **flashes**, the in situ sensor is not justified.

## Ground truth

Nets, IFCB, HPLC pigments. Not CPUE.

## FP / FN

Electronics/light leaks; daytime; non-luminous biomass. Satellite FP: calling a dinoflagellate index bioluminescence.

## Legal / privacy / eco

Low. Strobe/stim review if listed turtles present.

## Economics

2. Cannot separate taxa classes.  
3. Color-only product labeled bioluminescence.  
4. No instrument access within 6 months.

## Scale path
