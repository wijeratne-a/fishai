# Observation plan — Atlantic goliath grouper

**Status:** Ranked ideas for later work. Not a field order.  
**Geography:** coarse only (southwest Florida shelf / Ten Thousand Islands / adjacent nearshore reef **class**). **No wreck names, no nursery pins, no secret sites.**

Labels: **supported** = mentioned on a fetched public page as a method already used; **speculative** = reasonable next measurement not evidenced on those pages.

---

## Ranked next measurements

| Rank | Measurement | Grain | Why it would reduce uncertainty | Label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Structured juvenile surveys** in mangrove / estuary shallows of the Ten Thousand Islands *region* (not a named creek) | Bay-scale cells; depth = shallows | NOAA describes 1997–2005 juvenile mark-recapture there. Repeatable effort gives **non-detections**, which compiled OBIS lacks. | **Supported** (method class) |
| 2 | **Offshore visual / drop-camera / BRUV on a coarsened reef grid** outside spawning pinnacles | ≥ several km cells; adult 0–50 m band | Occupancy with effort; avoids publishing wrecks. | **Supported** as visual/tagging class (NOAA adult work); site list **speculative** |

| 4 | **Passive acoustics** on a regional grid (swim-bladder rumble) | Regional receivers; no localization of a named wreck | Florida Museum documents the sound. Could flag calling without a pin map. False positives unquantified. | **Speculative** as a network; sound **supported** |

## Explicitly not in this plan

- Publishing exact aggregation coordinates “for science users.”
- Using AIS to find fish.
- Treating Copernicus SST as a goliath sighting.
- A harvest or “safe to spear” product.

Until effort-aware data exist, the honest issued answer remains **unknown** plus optional **historical pattern**.
