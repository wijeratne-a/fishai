    Learn

    Measured means someone recorded a value (for example water temperature).

    Guessed means a model inferred something. It is not a sighting.

    Future means a forecast for a named time window. It is not live tracking.

    Don’t know (stripes) means we do not have an estimate. It does not mean the ocean is empty. Unknown is the default scientific status.

    This globe does not collapse answers into “where the fish are.” Observed presence, occurrence probability, relative abundance, movement, habitat suitability, and unknown stay separate. Habitat is favorable conditions — not confirmed presence. Current estimates and forecasts stay off unless a model card is actually published.

    The Willapa Bay oyster screen is a working-conditions demo. It is farm air, tide, and waves — not oyster GPS. Searching yellowfin tuna (or most names) should say we have no current location and no forecast.

    A species page may show past reports from public databases. Those are a historical pattern, not where the animals are now. Sensitive taxa (for example white shark) withhold locations.
