# Pricing Hypothesis — FishAI

**Date:** 2026-09-18  
**Status:** Hypothesis only. No interviews, no closed deals. Do not treat prices as market-cleared.

**Principle:** Price the **decision + private loop**, not “access to ocean data.” NANOOS, WDOH, SoundToxins, NWS, and ODFW reports are free. If we price like a consumer fishing app, we lose. If we price like enterprise aqua-software before proving lift, we do not get a first farm.

---

## 1. Packaging (recommended wedge)

**Product name (working):** FishAI Ops-Risk Brief — Pacific oyster, Washington, 72h  
**Unit of sale:** one farm account, covering a **named set of leases**  
**Delivery:** daily email + PDF (seasonal pause optional in deep winter if the farm wants it)

| SKU | What they get | Hypothesis price | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Design-partner pilot (8 weeks)** | Daily brief for **1 primary + up to 2 extra leases**; weekly calibration note; outcome form; private logbook; founder Slack/email. **No public case study without written OK.** | **$0** (preferred) or **$1** token invoice, **or $500** if they insist on paying to take it seriously | First 3 farms; outcome logging **required** |
| **Paid pilot (90 days)** | Same, plus 1 mid-pilot wording review, 1 end report (their data only) | **$750–$1,500** for 90 days (1–3 leases) | Farms that used the 8-week briefs |
| **Season ops (May–Oct)** | Daily briefs through heat/storm season | **$4,500–$8,000 / farm** (up to 3 leases) | Natural budget cycle |
| **Recurring** | Monthly, cancel anytime after 90 days | **$400–$900 / farm / month** (up to 3 leases) + **$150–$250 / extra lease** | If they want year-round |
| **Enterprise / multi-site** | 10+ leases, GIS/API later, SSO later | Custom **$1,500–$5,000 / month** | Taylor-class; only after 2+ smaller farms pay |

**Not in v0 price:** sensors, food-safety, insurance certificates, public maps, crew scheduling.

## 2. Anchor points (public comparables — not our price)

These are **anchors**, not claims that we are “like” them.

| Comparable | Public signal | Implication |
|---|---|---|
| NANOOS NVS Shellfish Growers | Free IOOS portal | We cannot charge for “a map of temperature.” |
| WDOH temp network + closure viewer | Free, official | We cannot charge for harvest legality. |
| SoundToxins | Partnership / public-good HAB | Adjacent; food-safety. Do not compete; link. |
| BlueTrace (ex-OysterTracker) | Paid farm ops/traceability; launch era had paying farms | Growers **do** pay software, for inventory/compliance, not 72h stress. |
| Esri for a large farm | Enterprise GIS | Too heavy; a large farm may already have GIS — we should **feed** it later, not replace it. |
| aquaManager / Innovasea / Aquabyte | Quote-based; often **$10k–$50k/yr** class (third-party estimates; vendors rarely list prices) | Finfish/shrimp production software. Different buyer motion. |
| Manolin | Historical **NOK 10,000/yr** essentials (Norway salmon, ~2019 reporting); Observer reports cited **from $995**; free public-data tier | Shows aqua buyers accept **subscription risk scores** — in finfish, with dense private data. |
| Fishbrain Pro | **$12.99/mo or $79.99/yr** | Consumer fishing ceiling. Do not use for oyster B2B. |
| FishTrack Premium | **~$14.99/mo or $95.99/yr** | Same. |
| RipCharts | **$99–$169/yr** | Satellite charts. |
| Hilton’s Realtime Navigator | **$200/yr** per region | Satellite charts. |
| Vericatch ELOG | **~$60–$65/yr** (Canada compliance) | Compliance is cheap; intelligence can be dearer — only with proof. |
| Charter trip value | Hundreds–thousands per trip | High pain, low software WTP historically. |

**Oyster price band $400–$900/mo** sits **above** consumer fishing apps and **below** full farm-management suites. That is intentional: we sell a **daily decision brief**, not an ERP.

---

## 3. Willingness-to-pay hypothesis (to test, not assert)

A WA farm should pay if **one** of these is believed after the pilot:

1. **One avoided bad-handling window** per season is worth more than the subscription (labor + product quality).
2. **Crew allocation across leases** improves enough to save ≥1 wasted skiff day per month.
3. The private log + briefs are useful for **insurance, lenders, or internal QA** even if the score is only “as good as a careful NANOOS user.”
4. They want a **human-QA’d, lease-specific sentence** they can forward — not another portal.

**Kill prices:** if they offer $20/month “like an app,” we are in the wrong category. Either the brief is not decision-grade, or we are talking to the wrong person (crew instead of P&L owner).

## 5. Incentives to contribute data (all wedges)

Price **without** outcomes is a dead product. Build incentives into the SKU:

| Incentive | Mechanic |
|---|---|
| **Pilot requirement** | Design-partner: briefs continue only if weekly outcome completeness ≥ **60%** of work days |
| **Credit** | Paid: **$50–$100 / month bill credit** if completeness ≥ 80% |
| **Private analytics** | Only outcome-loggers see “your Elevated days vs your reported hard-work days” |
| **Priority** | Complete loggers get first access to extra leases / next species |
| **Co-op bargain** | Association deal: discounted seats if members contribute coarsened, delayed, opt-in data to a **non-public** research file |

Never: public leaderboards, public mortality, public spots, selling their data to neighbors.

## 6. Cost-to-serve (so price is not fantasy)

| Phase | Cost-to-serve (order of magnitude) | Price implication |
|---|---|---|
| Manual 3-farm pilot | Founder time; ~2 hours/day | Price is a **signal**, not margin |
| 10 farms, still semi-manual | Need part-time assembler or automation of pulls | $400/mo barely covers if QA is human |
| 30+ farms | Must automate score + template; QA sample | Recurring can work |

If we cannot automate source pull by farm 8, **do not add farms**. Raise price or stop.

## 8. Experiments (30/90 days)

1. **Van Westendorp-lite in interviews** (too expensive / bargain / stretch / too cheap) — 15 growers. Do not average into a fake “market price.”
2. **Take-it-or-leave-it paid pilot** at $1,000 / 90 days to the first farm that used free briefs.
3. If 0/3 design partners will pay anything after 8 weeks, **price is not the first failure** — the job-to-be-done failed. Pivot memo, do not discount to $9.99.
